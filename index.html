CYBERSECURITY TRAINING

Phishing Attacks

Learn how to identify suspicious emails, fake login pages, fraudulent websites and social engineering attacks.


🛡️ STOP • CHECK • VERIFY

01

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyberattack where criminals pretend to be a trusted person, company or service to trick you into giving away sensitive information.

  • 🔑 Passwords and usernames
  • 💳 Banking and card information
  • 📱 OTPs and verification codes
  • 👤 Personal information
  • 💰 Money
02

How to Spot a Suspicious Email

📧 Check the Sender

Look carefully at the email address. Fake addresses often contain small spelling changes.

🚨 Urgent Messages

Be suspicious of messages demanding immediate action.

🔗 Check Links

Hover over links and inspect the real destination before clicking.

📎 Attachments

Do not open unexpected attachments, especially from unknown senders.

03

Example: Phishing Email

From: security@paypa1-support.example


Your account has been locked!

⚠️ You must verify your account within 30 minutes or your account will be permanently deleted.


Click here to verify your account immediately.


🚨 Red flags:

  • Urgent threat
  • Suspicious sender address
  • Unexpected login request
04

Fake Login Pages

  • 🔍 Carefully check the website address.
  • ✏️ Look for spelling mistakes in the domain.
  • 🔐 HTTPS does NOT automatically mean the website is legitimate.
  • 🚫 Avoid logging in through links received unexpectedly.
  • 🌐 Open the official website yourself instead.

Remember!

A website can look exactly like the real one and still be designed to steal your password.

05

Fraudulent Websites

🌐 Strange Domain

The website address does not match the real organization.

💸 Unrealistic Offers

Huge discounts, prizes or rewards can be bait.

⏱️ Countdown Timers

Fake urgency is commonly used to pressure victims.

⚠️ Pop-ups

Unexpected warnings asking for downloads or personal information are suspicious.

06

Social Engineering Techniques

  • Impersonation: Pretending to be a bank, teacher, boss or friend.
  • Urgency: Pressuring you to act immediately.
  • Fear: Claiming your account or money is in danger.
  • Reward: Offering prizes, refunds or free gifts.
  • Authority: Pretending to be an important official.
  • Curiosity: Sending shocking or tempting links.
07

Case Study: Fake Bank Alert

Scenario

You receive a message saying: "Suspicious activity detected. Verify your bank account immediately."


What are the warning signs?

Urgency + unexpected message + login link + suspicious website.


Safe Response

Do not click the link. Open your bank's official app or website yourself and check your account.

08

Real-Life Example: CEO Fraud

A criminal may pretend to be a company manager and ask an employee to urgently transfer money or buy gift cards.


  • 🚨 The request is unusual.
  • ⏱️ The attacker creates urgency.
  • 🤫 They may ask the employee not to tell anyone.
  • 📞 The request should be verified through another trusted method.
09

How to Stay Safe

  • 🛑 Stop before clicking.
  • 🔍 Check the sender and website address.
  • 🔐 Use strong, unique passwords.
  • 📱 Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
  • 💻 Keep your software and browser updated.
  • 🚫 Never share passwords, PINs or OTPs.
  • 📢 Report suspicious messages.
